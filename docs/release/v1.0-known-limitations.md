## 1. Architectural Scope & Release Intent

Tavoryn Terminal Research Edition v1.0 is engineered specifically for **quantitative analysis, macroeconomic surveillance, fundamental valuation, technical research, and risk modeling**. It is intended to be run locally by financial analysts, researchers, and algorithmic traders.

It is **not** a multi-tenant web application, a hosted cloud broker, or an ultra-low-latency direct-market-access (DMA) execution platform.

---

## 2. Live Trading & Broker Connectivity Fencing

### A. Intentional Phase 10 Freeze (Paper Trading Only)
- In accordance with the Phase 9 baseline governance rules, **Phase 10 (Multi-Broker Live Order Routing) has not been implemented and remains frozen**.
- Built-in trade execution is strictly confined to **Simulated Paper Trading** (`lib/paper-trading.js`).
- Order placement routes (`POST /api/trad/order`) update the local simulated account ledger in `data/paper-account.json`. They do not connect to live broker gateways, credit facilities, or real money clearing houses.

### B. Live Capital Fencing
- The `/api/market-data/health` endpoint explicitly reports:
  ```json
  "brokerIsolation": {
    "isLiveBrokerConnected": false,
    "liveOrdersPermitted": false
  }
  ```
- Deploying capital with live market counterparties requires external, user-authorized connectors or dedicated command-line utilities (e.g. PTQ), which operate outside the web server boundary.

## 3. Market Data & Feed Limitations

### A. Public Unauthenticated Data Sources
- Tavoryn Terminal utilizes unauthenticated public financial endpoints (Binance, Coinbase, Yahoo Finance, Nasdaq, Fair Economy, Kraken) to provide comprehensive zero-cost market coverage without requiring paid Bloomberg or FactSet terminal subscriptions.
- **Rate Limits & Throttling:** Upstream providers may occasionally enforce HTTP 429 rate limits during intense market volatility (e.g. Fair Economy economic calendar). The terminal handles these gracefully with fallback sources and cached data, but brief latency spikes may occur.
- **Data Licensing & Redistribution:** Market quotes and company filings are pulled on-demand for local personal research and must not be re-hosted or redistributed commercially without upstream provider licenses.

### B. Market Depth & Fixed Income Instruments
- **Level 2/3 Order Book Depth:** Full exchange order books (e.g. NASDAQ TotalView, CME BookPath) are not available via public scraping endpoints. Crypto order books are sourced via Binance/Coinbase public depth snapshots (top 100 levels).
- **Single Corporate Bonds & OTC Derivatives:** Individual illiquid corporate bond dealer quotes and single-name Credit Default Swaps (CDS) are not traded on public centralized exchanges. Tavoryn Terminal models credit risk using ICE BofA Option-Adjusted Spread (OAS) benchmark indices and benchmark US Treasury par yield curves.

### C. Historical Bar Depth
- Daily bars for major equities and cryptocurrencies extend back several years.
- Intraday high-resolution bars (1-minute, 5-minute) are limited to the lookback windows retained by public providers (typically 30 to 60 days on Yahoo and Binance).

## 4. Quantitative Modeling Assumptions & Constraints

### A. Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Engine
1. **Gordon Growth Boundary Singularity:**
   - The Gordon Growth formula $TV = \frac{FCF_N \cdot (1 + g_{term})}{WACC - g_{term}}$ mathematically requires $WACC > g_{term}$.
   - If an analyst inputs a discount rate $WACC \le g_{term}$, the engine rejects the calculation.
   - In the 5x5 sensitivity matrix, cells where $WACC \le g_{term}$ display `"N/A"`.
2. **Perpetual Growth Assumption:**
   - The standard DCF model assumes stable, long-term mature earnings. It is not suitable for early-stage unprofitable growth companies or biotech firms without manually adjusting projected cash flows or terminal multiples.
3. **Financial Statement Units:**
   - SEC filings sourced from the Nasdaq API report financial statements in thousands ($\$k$). The engine normalizes these by dividing by 1,000 to convert to millions ($\$M$). For foreign companies reporting under non-USD currencies or foreign accounting standards (e.g. IFRS ADRs), users should verify statement units.

### B. Portfolio Stress Testing Engine
1. **Linear Factor Sensitivity:**
   - The portfolio stress testing engine models asset price movements using linear market beta sensitivities:
     $$r_i = \beta_i \times r_{\text{market}} + \Delta_{\text{sector}} + \Delta_{\text{asset}}$$
   - While effective for systemic historical drawdowns (e.g. 2008 GFC, 2020 COVID crash), linear approximations may underestimate extreme tail risks during non-linear liquidity spirals or short squeezes.
2. **Zero-Beta Cash Assumption:**
   - Cash holdings are treated as completely immune to equity and bond market shocks ($\beta = 0$). In hyper-inflationary crises, currency purchasing power degradation is not modeled as a nominal equity drawdown.

### C. Algorithmic Backtesting
1. **Execution Queue Realism:**
   - The backtester models bar-level execution (next-bar open) with configurable slippage and commissions. It does not simulate full L2 order queue priority, cancel-replace microsecond races, or exchange latency arbitrage.
2. **Survivorship Bias:**
   - Historical stock screening and backtests evaluate currently listed securities. Delisted companies from past decades are not included in free public tickers.

## 5. Security & Workstation Boundaries

1. **Single-User Local Architecture:**
   - The terminal is designed for local desktop execution. It does not provide multi-tenant user accounts, hashed passwords, or role-based access control (RBAC).
   - Binding the application to a public IP address (`HOST=0.0.0.0`) on an untrusted network is strongly discouraged unless protected by a reverse proxy with TLS and mutual authentication.
2. **Browser JavaScript Memory Heap:**
   - Heavy quantitative workflows (e.g. backtesting 500,000 historical bars across 50 strategies simultaneously) may approach standard browser JavaScript heap limits (~2 GB in V8). Large backtesting sweeps should be executed modularly.

## 6. Summary Disposition

These limitations reflect deliberate, institutional architectural boundaries designed to guarantee zero runtime dependencies, capital safety, and complete operational transparency.
