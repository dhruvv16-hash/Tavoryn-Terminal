# Tavoryn Terminal Research Edition v1.0 — Known Limitations & Operational Boundaries

**Release Target:** Tavoryn Terminal Research Edition v1.0  
**Baseline:** Phase 9 Frozen Baseline  
**Scope:** Declared Limitations, Boundaries, and Exclusions  

---

## 1. Upstream Public Data Provider Dependencies

1. **Exchange Feed Delay:** Public equity, index, and commodity quotes are retrieved from free public gateways (e.g. Yahoo Finance, Nasdaq) and may experience delays up to 15 minutes during standard market trading hours. They are not paid direct-market-access (DMA) consolidated SIP feeds.
2. **Off-Market Hours:** During weekends and holidays, equity price quotes reflect the last official close and do not stream live bid/ask spreads.
3. **Public Gateway Throttling:** Rapid, automated high-frequency polling can encounter upstream IP rate limits. The terminal employs exponential backoff, but users should avoid automated scraping.

---

## 2. Research & Simulation Scope Boundaries

1. **Non-Live Broker Fencing:** Research Edition v1.0 operates under an intentionally fenced non-live execution policy. Live broker order routing, production API keys, and real capital deployment are disabled.
2. **Simulated Paper Trading:** All order entry executed in `TRAD` is recorded strictly within the local simulation ledger (`data/paper-account.json`). Orders do not impact real-world liquidity or exchange order books.
3. **Backtesting Generalization Disclaimer:** Historical backtest results (`BTST`) are hypothetical simulations based on past price action. Positive backtest results do not guarantee future profitability and do not account for extreme liquidity vacuums or structural market regime breaks.

---

## 3. Quantitative Risk Modeling Boundaries

1. **Portfolio Stress Testing Simplifications:** The `STRESS` model applies linear factor shocks based on equity market beta ($\Delta V = V \times \beta \times \text{Shock}$). It does not model nonlinear derivatives exposure (Option Gamma, Vega, Charm), cross-asset correlation breakdowns, margin calls, or forced liquidation cascades.
2. **DCF Valuation Boundary:** In the Gordon Growth perpetuity model, if user input $WACC \le g$, the engine halts terminal calculation and flags the inputs as mathematically non-converging. Standard DCF requires positive operational cash flows; negative cash flows reflect operational cash burn.

---

## 4. Hardware & Platform Calibration

1. **Operating System Support:** Fully verified on Windows 11 (x64) and Linux/macOS using Node.js v18 and higher.
2. **Filesystem Durability:** State persistence utilizes atomic rename with bounded retry to handle transient Windows antivirus file locks. High-concurrency multi-user write scenarios on shared network drives (SMB/NFS) are not supported.
