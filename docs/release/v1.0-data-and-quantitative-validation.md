## 2. Market Data Infrastructure & Provider Normalization

### A. Multi-Provider Fallback Architecture
The terminal integrates six independent, unauthenticated public financial feeds without requiring proprietary broker credentials:
1. **Binance API:** Primary cryptocurrency spot candles (BTC, ETH, SOL) and real-time order books.
2. **Coinbase API:** Secondary cryptocurrency failover source.
3. **Yahoo Finance:** Global indices, international equities, foreign exchange crosses (FXC), and intraday commodity prices.
4. **Nasdaq API:** US equities, market screeners, official corporate profiles, and SEC financial statement metrics.
5. **Fair Economy / ForexFactory:** Real-time macroeconomic economic calendar events and consensus estimates.
6. **Kraken API:** Fiat-crypto cross rates and institutional foreign exchange benchmarks.

### B. Data Normalization & Pipeline Hygiene
Raw upstream ticks and bar arrays are processed through `MarketDataPipeline` (`lib/market-data-pipeline.js`) to enforce structural invariants:
- **OHLC Integrity:** High must be the supremum ($\text{High} \ge \max(\text{Open}, \text{Close})$) and Low must be the infimum ($\text{Low} \le \min(\text{Open}, \text{Close})$). Inverted bars from upstream feeds are corrected.
- **Monotonic Timestamps:** Timestamp sequences are verified strictly ascending ($t_i > t_{i-1}$). Duplicate timestamps are merged.
- **Non-Negative Spreads:** Bid/ask spreads where $\text{Ask} < \text{Bid}$ are discarded as crossed quotes.
- **Bar Resampling:** Higher timeframes (e.g. 1W, 1M) are resampled from daily bars with volume summation and open-to-close preservation (`lib/series.js`).

## 3. Technical Indicator Library (87 Verified Indicators)

The terminal contains a built-in library of 87 technical indicators (`public/lib/indicators.js`). In `test/reference.test.js`, every indicator is evaluated against 400 historical reference bars identical to TradingView reference outputs.

### Indicator Categories & Representative Formulations

#### 1. Trend & Moving Averages
- **Exponential Moving Average (EMA):**
  $$\alpha = \frac{2}{N + 1}, \quad \text{EMA}_t = \alpha \cdot P_t + (1 - \alpha) \cdot \text{EMA}_{t-1}$$
- **Volume-Weighted Average Price (VWAP):**
  $$\text{VWAP}_t = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^t P_i \cdot V_i}{\sum_{i=1}^t V_i}, \quad P_i = \frac{\text{High}_i + \text{Low}_i + \text{Close}_i}{3}$$
- **Hull Moving Average (HMA), Double EMA (DEMA), Triple EMA (TEMA), Arnaud Legoux (ALMA).**

#### 2. Momentum & Oscillators
- **Relative Strength Index (RSI):**
  $$U_t = \max(P_t - P_{t-1}, 0), \quad D_t = \max(P_{t-1} - P_t, 0)$$
  $$\text{RS} = \frac{\text{RMA}(U, N)}{\text{RMA}(D, N)}, \quad \text{RSI} = 100 - \frac{100}{1 + \text{RS}}$$
- **Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD):**
  $$\text{MACD Line} = \text{EMA}_{12}(P) - \text{EMA}_{26}(P), \quad \text{Signal Line} = \text{EMA}_9(\text{MACD Line})$$
  $$\text{Histogram} = \text{MACD Line} - \text{Signal Line}$$
- **Stochastic, CCI, Williams %R, Fisher Transform, Connors RSI, Ultimate Oscillator, TRIX.**

#### 3. Volatility & Bands
- **Bollinger Bands:**
  $$\text{Middle} = \text{SMA}_N(P), \quad \text{Upper/Lower} = \text{Middle} \pm k \cdot \sigma_N(P)$$
- **Average True Range (ATR):**
  $$\text{TR}_t = \max(\text{High}_t - \text{Low}_t, \, |\text{High}_t - \text{Close}_{t-1}|, \, |\text{Low}_t - \text{Close}_{t-1}|)$$
  $$\text{ATR}_t = \text{RMA}(\text{TR}, N)$$
- **Keltner Channels, Donchian Channels, Chande Kroll Stop, Supertrend.**

#### 4. Volume & Order Flow
- **On-Balance Volume (OBV):**
  $$\text{OBV}_t = \text{OBV}_{t-1} + \begin{cases} V_t & \text{if } P_t > P_{t-1} \\ 0 & \text{if } P_t = P_{t-1} \\ -V_t & \text{if } P_t < P_{t-1} \end{cases}$$
- **Chaikin Money Flow (CMF), Accumulation/Distribution (A/D), Ease of Movement (EOM), Volume Profile (VPVR).**

## 4. Backtesting & Deterministic Replay Engine

### A. Elimination of Lookahead Bias
The simulation engine (`DeterministicReplayEngine` in `lib/replay-engine.js`) enforces that trading signals generated at bar $t$ are evaluated strictly using information available at or before bar $t$. Execution occurs on the open of bar $t+1$:
$$t_{\text{signal}} \le t_{\text{bar}}, \quad t_{\text{fill}} = t_{\text{bar}+1}.\text{open}$$
Verified in `test/backtesting-hardening.test.js` via `verifyNoLookaheadBias`.

### B. Execution Realism: Slippage & Transaction Costs
1. **Slippage Model:**
   $$P_{\text{fill}} = P_{\text{expected}} \cdot (1 \pm \text{slippageBps} / 10000)$$
2. **Transaction Costs:**
   $$\text{Commission} = \max(\text{minTicketFee}, \, \text{Notional} \cdot \text{commissionBps} / 10000)$$

### C. Performance & Risk-Adjusted Metrics
`computeBacktestMetrics` (`lib/backtest-metrics.js`) calculates comprehensive statistical measures:
- **Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR):**
  $$\text{CAGR} = \left(\frac{\text{Ending Equity}}{\text{Starting Capital}}\right)^{\frac{1}{\text{Years}}} - 1$$
- **Sharpe Ratio:**
  $$\text{Sharpe} = \frac{\bar{R}_p - R_f}{\sigma_p} \cdot \sqrt{\text{Periods Per Year}}$$
- **Sortino Ratio:**
  $$\text{Sortino} = \frac{\bar{R}_p - R_f}{\sigma_{\text{downside}}} \cdot \sqrt{\text{Periods Per Year}}, \quad \sigma_{\text{downside}} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{M}\sum \min(R_t - R_f, 0)^2}$$
- **Maximum Drawdown (MDD):**
  $$\text{MDD} = \max_{t} \left(\frac{\text{Peak}_t - \text{Equity}_t}{\text{Peak}_t}\right)$$
- **Calmar Ratio:** $\text{CAGR} / \text{MDD}$.
- **Profit Factor:** $\sum \text{Gross Profits} / \sum \text{Gross Losses}$.
- **Zero-Trade / Zero-Loss Safety:** Returns `null` or `0` gracefully when trades count is 0 or losses count is 0, avoiding `NaN` or `Infinity`.

## 5. Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Valuation Engine

### A. Mathematical Formulation
1. **Projected Free Cash Flows ($FCF_t$):**
   $$FCF_t = FCF_0 \times (1 + g)^t, \quad t \in [1, N]$$
2. **Present Value of Projected Cash Flows ($PV_{\text{proj}}$):**
   $$PV_{\text{proj}} = \sum_{t=1}^N \frac{FCF_t}{(1 + WACC)^t}$$
3. **Terminal Value Gordon Growth Model ($TV$):**
   $$TV = \frac{FCF_N \times (1 + g_{term})}{WACC - g_{term}}$$
4. **Present Value of Terminal Value ($PV_{\text{TV}}$):**
   $$PV_{\text{TV}} = \frac{TV}{(1 + WACC)^N}$$
5. **Enterprise Value ($EV$):**
   $$EV = PV_{\text{proj}} + PV_{\text{TV}}$$
6. **Enterprise Value to Equity Value Bridge:**
   $$\text{Equity Value} = EV + \text{Cash \& Short-term Investments} - \text{Total Debt}$$
7. **Intrinsic Value Per Share:**
   $$\text{Intrinsic Value} = \frac{\text{Equity Value}}{\text{Diluted Shares Outstanding}}$$
8. **Margin of Safety ($MoS$):**
   $$MoS = \frac{\text{Intrinsic Value} - \text{Current Price}}{\text{Current Price}}$$

### B. Gordon Growth Boundary Enforcement
When $WACC \le g_{term}$, the denominator $WACC - g_{term} \le 0$ produces a mathematical singularity (infinite or negative terminal value).
- **Core Engine Enforcement:** The engine strictly validates $WACC > g_{term}$. If violated, the calculation throws a descriptive error rather than returning misleading values.
- **5x5 Sensitivity Matrix Handling:** In the sensitivity grid, any cell where $WACC \le g_{term}$ is explicitly labeled `"N/A"`, preserving visual and analytical integrity.

### C. Financial Statement Unit Scaling & Share Derivation
Upstream financial statement providers (e.g. Nasdaq API) report numbers formatted in thousands of dollars ($\$k$) as strings with currency signs, commas, and parentheses for negative values:
1. **Raw String Parsing (`parseNum`):**
   - `"$111,482,000"` $\to 111,482,000$
   - `"-12,718,000"` $\to -12,718,000$
   - `"($12,718,000)"` $\to -12,718,000$
2. **Unit Normalization ($/1000$ to Millions):**
   - Operating Cash Flow: $\$111,482,000 / 1000 = \$111,482\text{M}$
   - Capital Expenditures: $-\$12,718,000 / 1000 = -\$12,718\text{M}$
   - Free Cash Flow: $\$111,482\text{M} - \$12,718\text{M} = \$98,764\text{M}$
3. **Implied Diluted Share Derivation:**
   $$\text{Implied Shares} = \frac{\text{Market Capitalization } (\$M)}{\text{Share Price } (\$/\text{share})} = \frac{\$3,500,000\text{M}}{\$225.00} = 15,555.56\text{M shares}$$
   - Provenance tracking sets `sharesSource = "derived_from_market_cap"` and provides a transparent footnote in both API and UI.

## 6. Portfolio Stress Testing Engine

### A. Universal Signed Position Valuation
To support long, short, and cash holdings in a single unified equation:
$$V_{\text{stressed}} = V \times \max(0, 1 + r)$$
where $V$ is current position market value and $r$ is asset-specific return shock.

1. **Short Position Symmetry:**
   For a short position ($V < 0$), when the market suffers a crash ($r < 0$):
   $$\Delta P\&L = -V \times r > 0 \quad (\text{short position generates profit, hedging drawdown})$$
2. **Cash Shock Immunity:**
   Cash holdings have $\beta = 0$ and $r_{\text{cash}} = 0$, resulting in:
   $$V_{\text{stressed}} = V_{\text{cash}} \times \max(0, 1 + 0) = V_{\text{cash}} \quad (100\% \text{ capital preservation})$$
3. **Price Multiplier Non-Negativity:**
   The term $\max(0, 1 + r) \ge 0$ ensures prices cannot become negative even under theoretical hyper-deflationary shocks ($r < -1.0$).

### B. Accounting Identity Verification
Across all stress scenarios, the fundamental conservation identity is validated:
$$V_{\text{start}} + \sum_{i} \Delta P\&L_i \equiv V_{\text{stressed}}$$
Verified in `test/stress.test.js` to within $10^{-4}$ precision (`reconciles: true`).

## 7. Model Assumptions & Limitations Summary

| Model / Feature | Underlying Assumptions | Known Operating Bounds |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DCF Model** | Constant perpetual reinvestment rate; stable capital structure | Invalid when $WACC \le g_{term}$; highly sensitive to terminal growth assumptions |
| **Gordon Growth** | Perpetual steady-state earnings growth rate | Unsuitable for early-stage or negative FCF companies without custom adjustment |
| **Stress Testing** | Linear factor sensitivity based on historical beta | Tail correlations may increase non-linearly during systemic liquidity freezes |
| **Technical Indicators** | Standard price/volume inputs; stationary distributions | Past price action does not guarantee future directional certainty |
| **Backtester** | Historical tick/bar execution without queue jumping | Slippage assumes standard liquidity; illiquid assets may experience higher impact |
